# Sources for the historic recap shorts

Rule: every fact on screen needs at least one source; lap numbers and figures need two that agree, or the official result.
Where sources disagree, the video leaves the detail out (listed under "left out").

## Visual licence
- Car sizes and gaps between cars are exaggerated so the cars are readable; real gaps would be a few pixels.
- Liveries are plain researched team colours. Teams not researched (Benetton 1991, BAR, Red Bull, Sauber 2005) are drawn grey.
