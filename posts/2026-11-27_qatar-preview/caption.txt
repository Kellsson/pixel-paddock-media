QATAR GP · WEEKEND PREVIEW 🏁

Pirelli caps every tyre set at 27 laps in Lusail, two more than last year. With 57 laps to run, every driver has to stop at least twice. Safety car laps count towards the limit too.

The minimum front pressure is 29 psi, the highest ever set at the start of a race weekend. Pirelli brings its three hardest compounds: C1, C2 and C3.

Antonelli is already champion. Verstappen leads the fight for P2 by 37 points, with Hamilton, Russell and Norris split by three.

Lights out Sunday 19:00 in Doha / 16:00 UTC / 11:00 New York.
